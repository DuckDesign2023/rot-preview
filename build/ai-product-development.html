AI is powerful. Architecture matters more.

Connecting an application to a large language model is easy. Building an AI-powered product you can trust is much harder.

Production AI requires decisions around data security, model routing, governance, reliability, integrations, cost, user permissions and human oversight.

Our own NOVAlab architecture, for example, uses a governed multi-stage pipeline, isolated customer contexts, controlled model routing and an EU-resident infrastructure to make AI suitable for enterprise use. We bring the same architectural thinking into the products we build with our customers.
