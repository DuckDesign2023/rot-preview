The problem

Stop managing the gaps between systems.

Most project platforms manage tasks. But project-driven companies manage much more than tasks.

People Qualifications Subcontractors Documentation Safety Quality Budgets Capacity Customers Suppliers Compliance

WorkHub was built around that reality. Instead of forcing the organisation to stitch together multiple specialised tools, WorkHub connects the operational parts of a project in one environment.

Photo placeholder: a project manager on site, WorkHub open on a tablet.

What it covers

Run the entire project from one place.

Project management

Manage projects using dashboards, task lists, Kanban boards, Gantt timelines and calendars. Work through structured project stages rather than disconnected task lists. AI assistance can help monitor project health, allocate work and generate summaries.

Teams & capacity

See who is working on what. Track roles, skills, certificates and capacity. Plan workloads before key employees become the bottleneck.

Documents

Keep files connected to the project and work they belong to. Synchronise with SharePoint and OneDrive and collaborate without creating yet another isolated document archive.

Compliance & HSE

Turn HMS, SJA, deviations, incident reporting and quality assurance into active workflows with timestamped audit trails. Not PDFs forgotten in a folder. Not paperwork completed the night before an audit. Real operational processes.

Pipeline & sales

Connect what sales is promising with what the organisation actually has capacity to deliver. Track opportunities, weighted pipeline and projected resource requirements before the project even starts.

Finance & reporting

Keep project budgets and expenditure connected to project delivery. Bring financial and ESG reporting closer to the work that creates the numbers.

WorkHub includes scoped access for subcontractors and suppliers so external participants can work directly with the information relevant to them without getting unnecessary access to the wider organisation.
