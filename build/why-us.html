Why clients choose us

Choosing the right technology partner is not only about finding the right technical skills.

It is about finding a team that understands your business, stays close to the work and takes responsibility for delivering results.

At Red Orange Technologies, we combine local presence and leadership in Norway with strong technology and delivery capabilities across our two offices in India.

Local presence in Norway

Our Norwegian team works closely with clients from the first conversation through delivery and ongoing development.

You get direct access to people who understand your business environment, expectations and commercial priorities.

Strong technology capabilities in India

Our two offices in India give us access to experienced technology professionals across multiple disciplines and platforms.

This allows us to build flexible teams, access specialist expertise and scale capacity as client needs change.

Direct access to decision-makers

We believe important projects benefit from short lines of communication.

Our leadership is actively involved across Norway and India, helping clients make decisions faster and ensuring that responsibility remains close to the project.

Flexible by design

Technology needs change. Projects grow, priorities shift and new expertise becomes necessary.

Our delivery model is designed to adapt, whether you need an individual specialist, a dedicated team or support for a larger transformation initiative.

Business understanding before technology

The most advanced solution is not always the right solution. We start by understanding what the business needs to achieve and then determine how technology can help.

Our goal is not simply to deliver technology. It is to create measurable value from it.

Built for long-term partnerships

We want to become part of the way our clients solve technology challenges, not simply complete a project and disappear.

That requires transparency, consistent delivery and trust built over time.

PRIMA

The values behind how we work

PRIMA represents the principles that guide how we work with clients, colleagues and partners across countries and cultures.

They are not simply words on a wall. They define the behaviour we expect from ourselves.

P

Passionate

We care about the work we deliver. Passion means being engaged, taking ownership and continuously looking for ways to create better results for our clients and colleagues.

R

Respectful

Strong partnerships are built on respect. We value different perspectives, cultures and expertise, and we communicate openly and professionally with clients, colleagues and partners.

I

Integrated

We work as one team across locations, disciplines and technologies. By connecting technical expertise with business understanding, we create stronger solutions and a better client experience.

M

Meaningful

Technology should create meaningful outcomes. We focus on work that improves businesses, solves real problems and creates lasting value for the people and organizations we work with.

A

Adaptive

Technology changes quickly, and so do businesses. We stay flexible, responsive and open to new approaches, adapting our teams and solutions as client needs evolve.
