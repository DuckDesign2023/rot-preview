The problem

Salesforce shouldn't require a consultant every time you want to change something.

Traditionally, even relatively straightforward changes can end up in a development backlog.

A new object A Flow A validation rule A field An Apex class An automation A new business process

With NOVA for Salesforce, you describe what you need. The platform translates that intent into Salesforce configuration, automation or code. You review it. You stay in control. Then you deploy.

Expertise — «AI doesn't replace Salesforce expertise…»

Новая раскладка: теги «Where experience still decides» переезжают влево под текст, справа — иллюстрация. Сюжет: рутинные блоки NOVA обрабатывает сама, сложная конструкция (архитектура, интеграции, миграция, безопасность) остаётся специалистам. Ниже — как будет выглядеть секция и два кадра на выбор.

Раскладкас первым кадром для примера
Expertise

AI doesn't replace Salesforce expertise. It removes the bottleneck around it.

There will always be complex Salesforce projects. That work requires experience.

But much of the everyday work around Salesforce is structured, repetitive and increasingly suitable for AI-assisted development. NOVA handles more of that work so your specialists can focus on the decisions where specialists actually add value.
