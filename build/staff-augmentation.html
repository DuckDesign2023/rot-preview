How we work

Our 3-step delivery model

We work according to a 3-step delivery model that gives you, as a client, insight and access to resources so you can make the best choices for your company's projects. Working closely together makes the process simpler and faster for putting highly qualified resources in place that fit into your team.

01 You describe your IT needs

Describe the desired technology, experience level, project type, and expected duration.

02 We present relevant resources

Within 48 hours, we send you CVs for evaluation. Interviews with candidate resources are conducted by you as the client, if desired.

03 A collaboration agreement is signed

Fixed price per developer or external development team. Onboarding with the client and a Norwegian project manager. Working hours adapted to Norwegian needs. Security and GDPR are safeguarded — the agreement is followed up by our Norwegian company.
