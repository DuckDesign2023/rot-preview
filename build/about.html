Our story

From two founders to an international technology company

Our story started in India in 2011. Today, we combine strong technical capabilities in India with local presence and leadership in Norway, giving our clients access to experienced technology professionals while keeping communication, commercial understanding and decision-making close.

Red Orange Technologies was founded in 2011 by Amit Sharma and Prakash Kattoli.

What started with software development, integrations and web solutions grew steadily through long-term client relationships, technical expertise and a simple principle: deliver quality and take responsibility for the result.

Over the years, the company has expanded its capabilities, team and international presence. Today, we support businesses with software development, cloud solutions, Salesforce, integrations, data, AI and dedicated technology resources.

The company has also developed a strong presence in Norway, creating a closer connection between clients in Europe and our technology and delivery teams in India.

Leadership

Local presence. International leadership.

Our Norwegian office is home to Henning Børve Figenschou, Geir Aasen and co-founder Amit Sharma, bringing leadership, commercial understanding and close client dialogue together in Norway.

In India, co-founder Prakash Kattoli works closely with our local teams and delivery organization across our two Indian offices.

This combination gives Red Orange Technologies a strong local presence in Norway while maintaining close leadership involvement with our technology and delivery teams in India.

For our clients, that means direct access to decision-makers, clear communication and a delivery model where responsibility is shared across borders rather than handed over from one location to another.

One company. Two countries. Shared responsibility.

Working internationally should not mean creating distance.

Our model is built around close collaboration between our clients, our Norwegian team and our technology teams in India. This gives our clients the advantages of an international technology organization while maintaining direct access to people who understand their business, market and expectations.

We believe the best technology partnerships are built through involvement, transparency and accountability. That means staying close to the project, communicating clearly and taking ownership throughout the delivery.
